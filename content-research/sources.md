**Naming note (important):** the prominent butte north of Emmett formerly called
"Squaw Butte" has been officially renamed **Sehewoki'i Newenee'an Katete** in federal
documents and maps, one of five renamed features in Gem County. Use the current
official name, or describe it geographically. Do not reproduce the former name.
